# Darbhanga Launch Plan — 30 Days to 100 Businesses

**Market:** Darbhanga, Laheriasarai, Benipur, Baheri, Jale  
**Target:** 100 registered businesses in 30 days  
**Goal:** Free plan adoption → product-market fit → word-of-mouth growth

---

## Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)
**Target: 10 businesses signed up**

### Day 1-2: Local Scouting
- Walk markets in Darbhanga Sadar + Laheriasarai Bazar
- Identify 50 target businesses (salons, clinics, coaching centers)
- Take photos of shops with WhatsApp numbers displayed → shows they're WhatsApp-active
- Create a target list in spreadsheet

### Day 3-4: Demo Preparation
- Set up demo account: `demo-salon` or use `/business-success?type=coaching` (no signup)
- Prepare a 3-minute WhatsApp voice note demo script in Hindi
- Prepare a printed A4 flyer (Hindi) with QR code linking to `/city/darbhanga`
- Prepare a printed "Booking Link" card for shop boards
- Print activation code card: `PLUS90` for anchor partners (90 days Plus free)

### Day 5-7: First Outreach (Personal)
**Script for shop owners (Hindi WhatsApp message):**
```
नमस्ते! मैं [Name] हूँ। आपके {business_type} के लिए एक free WhatsApp booking system है जो 
customers को automatically reminder भेजता है। Setup में सिर्फ 5 मिनट लगते हैं। 
Free demo देखना है? https://wa-booking-web.vercel.app/business-success
City page: https://wa-booking-web.vercel.app/city/darbhanga
```
- Send to 20 businesses personally
- Target: 5 signups by Day 7

---

## Week 2: Proof of Concept (Days 8-14)
**Target: 25 businesses signed up**

### Onboarding First 10 (Personal Touch)
- Visit the first 5-10 signed-up businesses in person
- Help them:
  1. Create business + services
  2. Connect WhatsApp
  3. Print QR code (offer free printing)
  4. Test booking end-to-end
- Collect video testimonials (30-second WhatsApp video in Hindi)

### Referral Launch
- Tell happy early businesses: "5 businesses refer करें, 3 months free Pro"
- Give each business a referral code (businessId as UTM)

### Local WhatsApp Groups
- Join 5+ local business WhatsApp groups in Darbhanga
- Share the platform with proof (booking confirmation screenshot)
- Expected: 10-15 new signups from groups

---

## Week 3: Scaling Outreach (Days 15-21)
**Target: 60 businesses signed up**

### Category-Specific Campaigns

**Salon Campaign:**
- Find barber / salon owners' WhatsApp groups
- Message: "अब customers Google Calendar wait नहीं करते — WhatsApp से directly book करते हैं। Free platform: [link]"
- QR stickers for shop mirrors/windows

**Clinic Campaign:**
- Target clinics with patients waiting outside
- Message: "Patient queue को WhatsApp से manage करें। No more phone calls. Automatic reminders."
- Partner with medical stores nearby to distribute flyers

**Coaching Center Campaign:**
- Timing: Start of new session (June/July)
- Message: "Students की fees और attendance ek jagah. Parents ko WhatsApp reminder. Free system."
- Target: All coaching centers near colleges + DM students

### Social Media (WhatsApp Status)
- Daily WhatsApp Status: "Darbhanga का {salon name} अब WhatsApp से booking लेता है 📱"
- Tag business owners in Instagram/Facebook stories
- Use local hashtags: #DarbhangaBusiness #LaheriasaraiBazar

---

## Week 4: Growth & Retention (Days 22-30)
**Target: 100 businesses signed up, 50 active**

### Local Press + Influencers
- Contact local newspaper (Dainik Bhaskar Darbhanga edition) for a feature story
- Find 1-2 local YouTube/Instagram influencers (even 5K followers) for demo video
- Offer: "Featured business on city landing page" for prominent local businesses

### Business Association Outreach
- Contact Darbhanga Business Association / Chamber of Commerce
- Offer free bulk registration for member businesses
- Present at one business meeting (15-minute presentation in Hindi)

### Retention: First-Week Active User Campaign
- Check which businesses are active (WhatsApp connected + at least 1 booking)
- Call/WhatsApp businesses who signed up but didn't connect WhatsApp
- Offer 10-minute personal setup assistance

## Field Operations Workflow

### Daily Routine (for Field Agent)
```
Morning:
1. Check Super Admin dashboard → new signups overnight
2. WhatsApp follow-up with yesterday's prospects
3. Plan 5-10 new business visits

Afternoon:
1. Visit businesses (2-3 in-person setups)
2. Post WhatsApp status with success story

Evening:
1. Update spreadsheet with leads
2. Send follow-up to unresponsive leads
3. Respond to WhatsApp queries
```

### Setup Script (In-Person, 5 min)
```
Step 1: Open wa-booking-web.vercel.app/signup?ref=darbhanga&pack=salon on owner's phone
Step 2: Enter mobile number → OTP 1234 (dev stub)
Step 3: Darbhanga fast path: 2 steps (or full 7-step onboarding for other cities)
Step 4: Optional: enter activation code PLUS90 for Plus features
Step 5: Go to /app/whatsapp → scan QR
Step 6: Test: book one appointment from the public link
Step 7: Show WhatsApp confirmation notification
Step 8: Print QR code / share booking link via WhatsApp
```

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| WhatsApp account gets banned | Use business owner's own number (not a shared number) |
| Low WhatsApp adoption | Focus on pain point: "No more missed bookings" |
| Setup too complex | Personal in-person setup for first 20 businesses |
| Competition from Vyapar/JustDial | Emphasize: "This is WhatsApp-native, no app for customers" |
